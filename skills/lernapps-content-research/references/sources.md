# Verifying a second source

## Check by opening

- Exists: loads; no error, empty page, redirect to start or search.
- Is the article or video itself: no search result, category, list, disambiguation.
- Covers exactly this competency (resistance for "Ich kann den Widerstand berechnen", not electricity in general).

## Rules

- No weak matches: nothing fits → leave it out, say so in the plan.
- Video: check channel and title via the platform's metadata (YouTube: oEmbed), never a search result.
- Video loads only after a click (`no-request-before-click`).
- Note per source: address, exact title, "verified".
- Take facts, never sentences; name source and licence of what you take (`third-party-licence`).
