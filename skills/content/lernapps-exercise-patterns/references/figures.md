# Figures

- Shows what the text next to it says; caption: what to look at; `alt`: what it shows (`explainer-representation-explained`).
- Drawn from the given, never the answer (`explainer-figure-hides-answer`).
- Bundled; nothing from other servers before a click (`no-request-before-click`); others' images with source and
  licence (`third-party-licence`).
- Works at 360 px: trees top to bottom; too wide → table, or split by stage.
- Check each in its own image of `npx --no -- lernapps screenshots`, not in the page image.
- Same conventions (colours, labels, units, orientation) across the app; write them into the guide, "Figures".
- The same figures work in a quiz question (`material`, skill `lernapps-content-questions`): an image of `src/images/` with
  `alt`, `caption`, `source` and `licence`.

## Story scenes (younger learners)

- Only where the story has drawn children. Start from `story-characters.svg` in this folder (the two children of the French app): copy it,
  keep the style, change the children's looks to your own, change the scene. Write names, looks and style into the guide, "Figures". One scene per page, the characters
  in the same places, the words they say in the page's text.
