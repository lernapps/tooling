Tooling

How learning apps for lernapps.net are built, checked and published: guidance for AI assistants, templates and checks, and the shared workflows of all sites on GitHub.

  • GitHub, explained: where an app lives and is published, and which parts of GitHub the tooling uses, for creators who do not know GitHub
  • Prompt engineering: how we make the guidance work for the creator's assistant, the evals and their results
  • Rules: what every app in the app overview must meet, with id, scope, severity and where each rule is told and checked
  • Schema of the validation report (JSON Schema): what lernapps check reports in YAML
  • Schema of a quiz's question bank (JSON Schema): the questions and options of an app from the archetype quiz, in one or more languages, validated when the app is built
  • Schema of an explainer's content (JSON Schema): the app, chapters, pages with their blocks and exercises, and data files of an app from the archetype explainer, as YAML, validated when the app is built
  • Schema of the review verdict (JSON Schema): what the review agent judged on an app that asks to be listed, in YAML
  • Architecture (arc42): how the tooling is built that helps a person and their AI assistant build an app that collects nothing in secret and fits the app overview
  • Source on GitHub: the shared workflows (build, check, publish) and the presets for updates
  • llms.txt: the commands, guidance, skills, rules and schemas, for AI assistants
  • Documentation of lernapps.net: vision, organization and platform design (German)

lernapps.net is free and has no ads. It lives on your thanks.
