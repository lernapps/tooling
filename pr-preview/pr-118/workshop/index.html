A workshop, not a factory

A software factory takes people out of the line and turns specifications into output. lernapps.net is a Manufaktur: one person who knows their subject makes one learning app. An AI agent does the handwork, and programs hold the rules. The person keeps every decision that needs judgement.

Factory

Many products from one specification. Quality means the output matches the spec. People supervise the line.

Workshop (Manufaktur)

One app from one person's knowledge: their subject, their learners, their material. Quality means learners learn. The person decides; the tools make the rest repeatable.

What a developer gets

No toolchain to fight: a template that passes every check from its first commit, error messages that say what to fix, and the same checks in the hooks, in CI and at the listing.

What a manager gets

Rules with ids, enforced by programs at fixed points, not by trust. Every app shows where it deviates and why. Content quality stays a human judgement, on purpose.

Your attention

Explore: you are the expert Plan: one careful read Code: hardly any Commit: a look and a yes
1

Explore

What should learners be able to do?

You focus on

  • The goal, the learners, your own material
  • The mistakes your learners make
  • Whether the research is right

The agent

  • Interviews you, one topic at a time
  • Proposes the archetype and says what it cannot do
  • Researches the curriculum, opens every source it cites
  • Drafts the catalog entry

Supported by

  • Guidance plan template: interview topics, checkpoints
  • Skill lernapps-app: what every app must be, choosing the archetype
  • Runtime template READMEs: what each archetype can and cannot do
  • Skill lernapps-content-research: curriculum, competencies, sources
  • Skill lernapps-<archetype>-design: what to ask, how content maps

Checkpoint: research – you confirm what the app teaches

2

Plan

How will learners practise?

You focus on

  • One read: tasks, sketches, the content method
  • Yes, or what to change

The agent

  • Maps the content onto the archetype
  • Chooses an exercise pattern per competency
  • Sketches every screen
  • Cuts later increments into slices for parallel sub-agents

Supported by

  • Skill lernapps-exercise-patterns: patterns by subject, shared data
  • Skill lernapps-content-growth: slices for an increment
  • Guidance sub-agent brief: one slice per sub-agent

Checkpoint: confirm the plan – nothing is built before your yes

3

Code

Built locally, by the agent

You focus on

  • Nothing you must do
  • At most three questions only you can answer

The agent

  • Creates the app from the template
  • Writes the app's own content guide
  • Writes the content, commit by commit
  • Fixes every failed check from its message
  • Compares screenshots with the sketches

Supported by

  • CLI lernapps create, update: the app from its template, kept current
  • Skill lernapps-check: the CLI, failed checks, the preset
  • Skill lernapps-<archetype>: what to write and its notation
  • Skill lernapps-content-questions: options and feedback that teach
  • Lint Lint rules: e.g. no request to another server before a click
  • Runtime runtime: engine, accessibility, no-JS page, its own tests
  • Hook pre-commit: format, lint, types, architecture
4

Commit

Online at your yes

You focus on

  • The preview: is this the app you want?
  • Yes to publish
  • The one click only the repo owner can make

The agent

  • Sets up the repo and Pages
  • Opens the preview pull request, sees its checks green
  • Publishes one squash commit, watches the deploy
  • Opens the listing pull request from your fork

Supported by

  • Skill lernapps-github: repo, preview, publish, fork
  • Skill lernapps-commit: linear history, message from the plan
  • Skill lernapps-listing: the entry and its result
  • Hook pre-push: build, end-to-end tests
  • Action app check: the same checks in CI
  • Action app preview and app publish: the checked bundle
  • Action listing validation: the entry against the live app

Checkpoints: publish, list – the app goes online at your yes; the listing pull request is the review

In every phase Guidance AGENTS.md and the plan file: where the work stands, what comes next, where the agent had to guess CLI lernapps check: one command, the same result in hooks, CI and the listing
Then, in the catalog A review agent judges what no check can, against a rubric The platform owner reads the verdict and decides on the listing

What no machine decides

  • What the app teaches, and for whom.
  • Whether the content teaches correctly: no check and no review proves that.
  • Whether the app goes online, and under whose name.
