lernapps.net tooling

A workshop, not a factory

A software factory takes people out of the line and turns specifications into output. lernapps.net is a Manufaktur: one person who knows their subject makes one learning app, for their learners, from their own material.

An AI agent does the handwork and programs hold the rules. The person keeps every decision that needs judgement: what the app teaches, whether it is right, whether it goes online.

What runs through every phase comes first, then the four phases of the plan. Each box is a part of the tooling, names what it is built with and links to its place in the architecture. Hover over a box, or move to it with the keyboard, to read what it does for whom.

What you get from it

  • As a developerNo toolchain to fight: a template that passes every check from its first commit, messages that say what to fix, and the same checks in the hooks, in CI and at the listing.
  • As a managerRules with ids, enforced by programs at fixed points, not by trust. Every app says where it deviates and why. Content quality stays a human judgement, on purpose.

What no machine decides

  • What the app teaches, and for whom.
  • Whether the content teaches correctly: no check and no review proves that.
  • Whether the app goes online, and under whose name.
