# The explainer's part of a plan

`lernapps skills --archetype explainer` and `lernapps plan new` put each section below where the plan has the line
`<!-- archetype: <name> -->`. Not a file to read or copy by hand.

## explore

- [ ] Load skill `lernapps-explainer-design`. An explainer has the look of the runtime: write "fixed by the
      archetype" under the topic "Look and feel" of "Answers".

## explore-structure

- [ ] Write under "Notes" which competency goes on which page and which pages form a chapter, as "Map the content" of
      skill `lernapps-explainer-design` says. Per page, name the prerequisites the app does not practise.

## plan

- [ ] Add to "Content method" one line per page, as "Engagement" of skill `lernapps-explainer-design` says: the
      opening task, the change of activity, the reward, the story.

## code-start

- [ ] Load skill `lernapps-content-questions`. Read, with your file tool and whole,
      `.agents/skills/lernapps-explainer/references/blocks.md` and
      `.agents/skills/lernapps-explainer/references/exercises.md`. A foreign language: also
      `references/language-tasks.md` in the same folder. A component in the plan: also `references/components.md`.
- [ ] Decide whether the app needs `scope`, as "`scope`" in

## commit-start

- [ ] Load skill `lernapps-explainer-self-assessment`. Add the self-assessment as it says, or write under "Key
      decisions" why the app has none. Commit.

## increment-commit

      as it says. Commit.
