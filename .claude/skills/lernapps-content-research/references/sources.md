# Verifying a second source

## Check by opening

1. It loads: no error, no empty page, no redirect to a start or search page.
2. It is the article or video itself: no search result, category, list or disambiguation.
3. It covers exactly this competency: resistance for "Ich kann den Widerstand berechnen", not electricity in
   general.

## Rules

- No weak matches. Nothing fits → leave it out and say so under "Notes".
- Video: check channel and title through the platform's metadata (YouTube: oEmbed), never through a search result.
- A video loads only after a click (`no-request-before-click`).
- Note per source: address, exact title, "verified".
- Take facts, never sentences. Name source and licence of what you take (`third-party-licence`).
