# Review rubric: archetype quiz

Questions with options, feedback and explanations, in a scaffold that brings the engine; the creator supplies the
questions as data. Judge these items after those of `review/rubric.md`.

## Feedback explains the answer

```rubric
id: quiz-feedback-explains
```

Read the questions in the bundle and in the app's question data. A finding: an option whose feedback only says right
or wrong without saying why, or a question without an explanation. Quote the question.
