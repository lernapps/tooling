# Review rubric: archetype explainer

Many pages of explanation, pictures and exercises, every page readable without JavaScript. Judge these items after
those of `review/rubric.md`.

## Every topic has exercises

```rubric
id: topic-has-exercises
```

Open every topic page. A finding: a topic that only explains, with no exercise that practises what it explains on
the same page or one link away, or exercises whose solution learners cannot check (no solution, no feedback).
